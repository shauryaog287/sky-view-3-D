We do not collect your location, contacts or photos, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not sell personal data. Usage counts contain no user, device or IP identifiers. You can switch them off in Settings; we also honour Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control.

Card and UPI details are entered directly with Razorpay; we never see or store them.

Service providers

  • Cloudflare (hosting, API, database) — processes requests including your IP address.
  • Supabase (sign-in and account records) — only if you sign in.
  • Razorpay (payments) — only if you subscribe.
  • Google Maps Platform (Photorealistic 3D Tiles) and Cesium ion (terrain and imagery) — your browser downloads map tiles directly from them, so they receive your IP address and standard request data under their own privacy policies.
  • Flight and weather data are fetched by our servers; those providers do not receive your personal data.
  • Optional analytics provider ([Plausible / PostHog / none]) — anonymous, cookieless events only.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data, withdraw consent (e.g. turn off notifications in your browser) and complain to the Data Protection Board of India or your local authority. To delete your account and associated data, email [privacy@your-domain]. Our Grievance Officer is [name, email].

Children

SkyView 3D is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
